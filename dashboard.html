DE.Bluizz Forex Academy

Start with the core strategy and develop consistent habits.

Lesson 1 — Market Structure

Learn to identify swing highs, swing lows, support, resistance and the direction of the current market.

Lesson 2 — Breakout

Look for a clear candle close beyond a meaningful support or resistance level. Be cautious of false breakouts.

Lesson 3 — Retest and Confirmation

After a breakout, wait to see whether price retests the broken level and whether price action supports your trading idea.

Lesson 4 — Risk Management

Decide your stop-loss, position size and acceptable risk before entry. A good setup can still lose.

Lesson 5 — Trading Psychology

Keep a journal, follow your rules, accept losses and avoid taking trades merely because you want to be in the market.
