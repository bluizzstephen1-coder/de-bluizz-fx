DE.Bluizz Forex Academy

Build your understanding of the market and develop disciplined trading habits.

Lesson 1 — Market Structure

Learn to identify swing highs, swing lows, support, resistance, and the direction of the current market.

Lesson 2 — The Breakout

Look for a candle close beyond a meaningful support or resistance level. Be cautious of false breakouts and weak moves.

Lesson 3 — Retest and Confirmation

After a breakout, observe whether price retests the broken level and whether price action supports your trading idea.

Lesson 4 — Risk Management

Decide your stop-loss, position size, and acceptable risk before entering. Even a well-planned trade can lose.

Lesson 5 — Trading Psychology

Keep a journal, follow your rules, accept losses, and avoid entering trades simply because you want to be in the market.
