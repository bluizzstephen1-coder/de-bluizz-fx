Core De.Bluizz Strategy

Breakout + Retest

This is the strategy course area. The website is prepared to expand into a complete lesson with chart examples, rules, entry models and practice exercises.

Strategy workflow

1Identify a meaningful level.
2Wait for a decisive breakout.
3Do not chase the first move.
4Wait for the retest.
5Look for confirmation.
6Define SL, target and risk before entry.

Practice rule

Use a demo account while learning. Record every setup in a journal: level, breakout, retest, confirmation, entry, stop loss, target and result.
